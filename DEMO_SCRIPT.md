# Demo Video Script (2 minutes)

## 0:00-0:15 — Hook
"What if your video game characters traded real money while you slept?"

## 0:15-0:45 — Problem
- Trading is complex and stressful
- Most people lose money
- No gamification, no fun

## 0:45-1:15 — Solution
- Deploy AI disciple fleet
- Watch 3D space map with live sectors
- Real-time price ticker from Coinbase
- Fleet automatically trades USDC

## 1:15-1:45 — Features
- Onboarding for new players
- Achievements and daily quests
- Portfolio tracking with Sharpe ratio
- Global chat for intel sharing
- Leaderboard with real rankings
